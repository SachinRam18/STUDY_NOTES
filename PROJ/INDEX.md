## Key Interview Principles

1. **Own your projects.** Speak with confidence. You built these.
2. **Be precise about AI terms.** Know the difference between: utility-based AI vs RL vs LLM, RAG vs fine-tuning, LangGraph vs LangChain Agents.
3. **Never bluff.** If a detail is unspecified, say so and give the correct theoretical answer.
4. **Defend your design decisions.** Know WHY you chose each technology.
5. **Know the failure modes.** Every interviewer will ask "what if X fails?"
6. **Practice speaking, not reading.** Record yourself giving the 5-minute explanations.
